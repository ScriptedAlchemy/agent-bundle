Bound every retained copy of a Workbench route invocation's render stream under one render-history window — at most 256 events and 2 MiB serialized, always keeping the newest event and the newest `shell`/`replace`/`complete` event — applied alike to the live `GET /api/routes/invocations/<id>/stream` replay, the completed and cancelled envelopes, `GET /api/routes/invocations/<id>`, the terminal `final` message, the Workbench's live view, and the envelopes the Workbench synthesizes from runtime runs. Stream replay is paced by socket drain instead of queued, so a reconnect can take a whole retained window through a backpressured socket. The render child no longer returns the whole event stream over IPC and the compiled-route producer keeps only the `complete` document. Envelopes whose events were evicted carry a new optional `retention` field (`producedEvents`, `evictedEvents`, `evictedBytes`, `retainedBytes`), cancelled and failed envelopes keep the retained window and its latest document, compiled routes' progress reports now reach the Workbench as `progress` render events, and `document`, `result`, `outcome`, and `correlationId` are never truncated. (#715)
